# ShipWreck Card Game

ShipWreck is a card game where you start out with a small raft and you try to grab items floating by to feed yourself,
extend your raft, or build unique upgrades to your raft.

## Cards

There are several types of cards:
- Player cards
- Base raft cards (left and right)
- Raft extension cards
- Raft equipement upgrade cards
- Wreckage item cards
  - food items
  - resource items
- Wreckage event cards

### Player Cards

Each has a chracter with unique skills

- Professor - Worth 1 Rescue Point, Can construct with 1 fewer resource of any type, cost 1 food
- Mary Anne - Worth 1 Rescue Point, can reach 1 space further when choosing wreckage, cost 1 food
- Movie Star - Worth 2 Rescue Points, cost 1 food
- Gillagun - Worth 1 Rescue Point, Can skip food once per game, cost 1 food normally
- Millionaire - Worth 2 Rescue Points, Cost 2 food
- Million Heiress - Worth 2 Rescue Points, Cost 2 food
- Wilson (Vollyball) - Worth 1 Rescue Point, No food cost

Player cards are shuffled and delt to players. Remaining player cards are mixed into the wreckage cards.

### Raft Cards

All base raft and raft extension cards have one upgrade slot. The left and right raft cards are always at the outermost part of a players area.
A raft is extended by splitting two cards and inserting the new extension card.

All players have two base raft cards at the start. There is an unlimited number of extension cards (say 40 in the deck).

### Raft Equipement Upgrade Cards

On either the base raft card equipment slot or the raft extension equipement slot players can construct equipment upgrade cards that are currently available if they have the exact resource to do so. They trade in their resources (placed back in the box, not used in game anymore) and take the top equipment upgrade card and place it on an open slot.
They must have an open slot.

- 2 Fishing Poles - Gives 2 food per round, costs 1 wood, 1 rope, 1 wire
- 1 Telescope - Increases players reach one extra space when grabbing wreckage cards, costs 2 wood, 1 plastic, 1 wire
- 3 Auto Nets - Play can grab two wreckage cards each turn, costs 1 cloth, 1 rope, 1 wood
- 2 Steel cordage - Defends against a shark attack, costs 2 wire, 1 plastic
- 5 Rain catchers - Gives 1 food per round, costs 2 cloth, 1 wood

### Wreckage Item cards

- 30 Plastic
- 30 Wood
- 30 Rope
- 30 Cloth
- 30 Wire

### Wreckage Event cards

- Shark - Choose a player and an invesion or raft extension to attack
- Tiaphoon - All players lose an invension or raft extension
- Flying fish - Treat as extra food for one turn

### Setup

Player cards are delt. Wreckage cards are delt out so that each player has 6 wreckage cards. The remaining wreckage cards are delt face up in front of each player going around and around until none are left.

### Game Play

Optionally at any time a player may extend their raft by one card.

On each turn a player can do one of:
- place a player card in hand onto a raft slot
- pick up a wreckage card and place into their hand
- play an event card from their hand
- build the current equipment upgrade card and place onto a raft slot


### End of Game

Once all of the wreckage cards are gone, the game ends. Rescue points are totaled. The most rescue points determans the winner.
Tie breaker is settled first by length of raft and finally by total number of invensions. Lastly a tie is a tie.
